Jazz: dream contracts for the paint duet (`@pixerate/schemas/jazz`). `JazzDream` is an image of where a painting could go, made by an image model and kept across pauses as reference for the bandleader; it is never painted. Adds `JazzDreamRequest` and `JazzDreamResponse` (the bodies of `POST /api/agent/paint-dream`), `JazzDreamImage`, `JazzDreamElement` and `JazzDreamDropReason`, with JSON Schema and Swift types.
